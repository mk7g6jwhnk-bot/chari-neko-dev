# v202 Result-only independent research evaluation plan

- Adds a sealed future-holdout-only evaluation plan after v201 approval.
- Reuse of prior research-trial and limited-application races is forbidden.
- Minimum future holdout: 50 races; maximum: 200 (default 100).
- Cohort, eligibility/exclusion rules, metrics, success/failure criteria, source cutoff, and leakage guards are sealed before evaluation starts.
- Plan creation does not start evaluation and cannot affect prediction, calibration, purchases, or production.
