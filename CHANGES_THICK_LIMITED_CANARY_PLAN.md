# v152 Thick Limited Canary Plan

- Final promotion approval can only produce a sealed limited-canary plan.
- Exposure is capped at 10%.
- The validated cohort is locked; cohort substitution is rejected.
- Return rate, thick hit rate, main hit rate, support hit rate, and bet count are mandatory monitoring metrics.
- All rollback conditions from the final promotion review package are inherited exactly; deletion or substitution is blocked.
- At least 30 canary races are required by the plan.
- Any rollback breach requires immediate stop.
- A completed plan does not activate canary behavior; a separate manual activation review is still required.
- Production write and automatic promotion remain disabled.
